About Veloura

Veloura was founded with a simple idea: fashion should feel elevated, effortless, and accessible to everyone. From everyday basics to statement pieces, we curate clothing for Men, Women, Kids, and Infants that blends comfort with contemporary style.

This project was built as a demonstration full-stack e-commerce application showcasing product browsing, cart & wishlist management, authentication, checkout, and a simple admin panel.
